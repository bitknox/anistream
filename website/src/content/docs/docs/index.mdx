anistream is an anime streaming TUI written in Rust. Real image rendering in the terminal,
mpv for playback, pluggable everything.

:::note[Sources are external]
anistream hosts no content and ships no sources of its own, not even an index. The torrent side
is a transport with nothing to search until you supply an endpoint. Everything else is a provider
API or plugin you configure. What you point any of it at is your own decision, at your own risk.
:::

## Today

anistream is under active development. What is in the app now:

- Browsing, search and seasonal lists from AniList, with no account. When AniList cannot answer,
  metadata falls back to Tenrai and then to what was last cached (`[meta] fallback`). `Ctrl-F`
  filters a search by genre, year, season, format, status and sort.
- A half-finished episode stays on the home screen with its position, independent of the sync
  threshold. `anistream --continue` resumes it from the shell, and `--play <id>` plays a title's
  next unwatched episode.
- The airing calendar spans a week back and a week forward, and on launch one toast says what
  aired for shows you watch since you were last here.
- Torrent streaming plays while downloading, is seekable, and sits behind the VPN guard. You bring
  the indexer.
- [Playback](/docs/guides/player/) runs in mpv with the app's own controls, seekbar thumbnails, a
  skip button, Up Next and menus, and one window carries a whole binge. Upscaling uses ArtCNN by
  default or Anime4K if you ask for it, and how much runs is decided per file. `y` plays an episode
  in a Syncplay watch party instead.
- On the command line, `--stats`, `--search`, `--export`/`--import`, `--stream-url`, `--doctor`
  and more. `--search`, `--stats` and `--random` also take `--json`, and `--update` replaces the
  binary in place.
- Local history lives in SQLite. It is the source of truth and works with no account at all.
- Tracker sync covers AniList, MyAnimeList, Kitsu, Simkl and Trakt, through an outbox that
  survives being offline. Kitsu is on by default and needs one sign-in. `a` sets a title's list
  status and `*` rates it, and both sync the same way progress does.
- Filler and recap episodes are marked from AnimeFillerList, for the long-runners where it matters.
- A downloads screen lists what you have pulled down, and completed files get subtitles muxed in.
- `P` pins a source per title, the Sources overlay picks exact releases, and playback falls over to
  another stream when one produces no video.
- Discord presence is off by default, and it can show that something is playing without saying
  what.
- Provider plugins are sandboxed WebAssembly components in any WASI 0.2 language. A
  [plugin store](/docs/plugins/stores/) that someone you know runs lets you install their plugins
  and share your own.

## In development

- More sources, as plugins. The [plugin system](/docs/plugins/authoring/) is the path for new
  providers.
- Licensed services stay links. anistream will not circumvent DRM, so a licensed stream can only
  ever open in its own service. The title screen already lists where AniList says a title is
  available, and a provider can return a link that opens in that service rather than a stream for
  mpv.
