anistream hosts no content. It is a free, open-source client for watching and tracking anime, made
by volunteers and released under the MIT licence.

## What anistream does

anistream plays video from sources you add, keeps your watch history on your device, and syncs it
to the trackers you sign in to. It is built for media you own, servers you run, streams you have
the right to play, licensed services and tracker sync. Browsing uses AniList's public data.

## What it does not do

anistream supplies, recommends, ranks and curates no sources. It ships no plugin and no plugin
store, and it does not list, link to or review any of them. It never gets round DRM: a licensed
stream opens in the service that carries it.

## Sources you add

Add only sources you are entitled to use. A plugin is written by someone else and comes from a
plugin store that someone else runs. The store's owner chooses what it lists and is responsible
for it. A plugin's author is responsible for the sources it reaches and the rights it relies on.

## Services anistream talks to

AniList, MyAnimeList, Kitsu, Simkl and Trakt are connected through their public APIs for browsing
and sync. Trailers play in YouTube's own player. anistream is not affiliated with any of them.

## Contact

To report something on this website, in the repository or in a release file, write to
legal@anistream.tv with the address of what you are reporting and why. We read every message and
aim to answer within seven days. anistream cannot remove anything from a plugin store or a source
run by someone else: write to that store's owner, whose contact is on the store's page, or to the
service that hosts it. Reports about the code on GitHub can also go through GitHub's own process.
