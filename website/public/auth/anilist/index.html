Finish signing in to AniList in the anistream app

This page should have opened the anistream app on your Android device. If you can read it, Android hasn't linked anistream.tv to the app yet.

  1. Open Android's Settings › Apps › anistream › Open by default.
  2. Turn on "Open supported links" and allow anistream.tv.
  3. Go back to anistream and sign in to AniList again.

Nothing from this page is sent anywhere. Your AniList sign-in stays in this browser tab, and closing it discards it.
