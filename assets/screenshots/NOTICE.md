# Screenshots of the player

The images here and under `website/public/player/` show frames from
**こうしす！ #3.1「セキュリティに完璧を求めるのは間違っているだろうか」(Part 1)** by
[OPAP-JP contributors](https://opap.jp/), licensed under
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), with its
English and Japanese subtitles from
[Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:こうしす！_-3.1「セキュリティに完璧を求めるのは間違っているだろうか」(Part1-4).webm).
Sponsor logos that appear in the episode are not part of that licence.

The upscaling comparison (`player-upscaling-compare.webp` and `website/public/player/compare-*`)
shows a frame from
**こうしす！ #3.2「セキュリティに完璧を求めるのは間違っているだろうか (Part 2)」** by the same
contributors under the same licence, from
[Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:こうしす！_-3.2「セキュリティに完璧を求めるのは間違っているだろうか_(Part2-4)」.webm):
a 480p encode of its 1080p release, upscaled, beside the release itself.

The screenshots are modified: anistream's player is drawn over the picture, and the chapter
marks, episode list and sources shown were added for them. The comparison is cropped, and three
of its four frames are scaled down and upscaled again. They are shared under the same licence.
The rest of anistream is MIT.
